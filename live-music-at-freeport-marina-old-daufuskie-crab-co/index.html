    How it works

    Music every Tuesday through Saturday.

    Most afternoon sets run four hours, usually 1 to 5 PM. Thursday sets start a half hour earlier. Tuesday evening sets start at 6 PM and run until 9.

    A few dates are still to be announced. Check back as the schedule fills in, or call the island crew and we will tell you who is playing.
