    About the island

    A few hours here feels like time travel.

    Daufuskie Island sits between Hilton Head and Savannah and is reachable only by water. Spend the day and you'll see nature in its most pristine form, watch dolphins ride alongside the ferry, and end the afternoon with a sunset that people cross the country to see.

    Whether you're visiting from overseas or boating over from Bluffton for lunch, you're always welcome at Freeport Marina. There are no traffic lights, no bridges and no hurry.

    How it works

    • Loading begins at low tide at Broad Creek Marina. The barge departs as the water rises enough for safe navigation, so times shift every day.
    • Aggregate loads are accepted only when contained and covered in a vehicle, or in bulk landscaping bags a forklift can pick up.
    • Equipment that doesn't return the same day comes back at no extra charge on a space-available basis.
    • Schedule is subject to change with weather. Your safety and comfort come first.

    Policies

    • Cancel a confirmed reservation at least 72 hours before the scheduled departure. For a 9:00 AM Friday departure, notice is due by 9:00 AM Tuesday.
    • Cancellations with less than 72 hours notice are charged the full cost of the reservation.
    • Loads travel at the owner's risk. We are not responsible for damage unless it results directly from a U.S. Coast Guard related incident. Secure and protect your equipment before transport.

    Daufuskie Island Dream Weddings

    Say it under the live oaks.

    Daufuskie Island offers an unforgettable backdrop for your wedding: pristine nature, Lowcountry charm and sunsets over the water. Ferry your guests over, ride the island by golf cart, and celebrate at the Crab Company with a band on the deck.

    Bloody Point Lighthouse

    The historic 1883 light station on the island's southern tip, with the original keeper's dwelling preserved as a museum. A landmark setting for ceremonies, portraits and sunset photos.

    • Built 1882 to 1883 to guide ships on the Savannah River, first lit April 20, 1883
    • Moved inland in 1912; today the Bloody Point Lighthouse & Museum, open daily 10 AM to 4 PM
    • Gift shop with island specialties, including the Bloody Point Bloody Mary mix

    Silver Dew Winery

    Right beside the lighthouse, Silver Dew pours tastings in a laid-back Lowcountry setting. A natural stop for rehearsal dinners, welcome parties and afternoon celebrations.

    • Wine tastings next door to the historic lighthouse grounds
    • Managed together with the lighthouse, so both stops pair easily in one visit
    • An easy golf cart ride from Freeport Marina
