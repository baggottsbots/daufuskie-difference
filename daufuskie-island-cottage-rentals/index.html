    Good to know

    Cottage questions, answered.

    How do I book a color cottage?

    The Blue Cottage, Yellow Seaside Shack and Pink Cabin are booked exclusively through Airbnb. Choose your cottage on this page, tap its Airbnb button and complete your reservation there.

    How many guests does each cottage sleep?

    The Blue Cottage, Yellow Seaside Shack and Pink Cabin are each an entire cabin with 1 bedroom, 1 bed and 1 bath, and sleep 2 guests. The Blue Cottage has a queen bed, and the Yellow Seaside Shack and Pink Cabin each have a double (full) bed. For families and groups, the 4 Bedroom Retreat sleeps 8 and the 5 Bedroom Getaway sleeps 10.

    Are the cottages pet friendly?

    Yes. All five rentals are equally pet friendly. Pets are welcome in the Blue Cottage, the Yellow Seaside Shack, the Pink Cabin, the 4 Bedroom Retreat and the 5 Bedroom Getaway. Check each Airbnb listing for the latest pet details before you book.

    How do I get to the cottages?

    Daufuskie Island is boat access only, so book a ferry from Broad Creek Marina on Hilton Head Island. A golf cart rental is recommended for getting around once you arrive.

    What is close to the cottages?

    All three cottages are at Freeport Marina, steps from the Old Daufuskie Crab Company and the general store. The restaurant hosts live music on weekends.

    Who do I contact with questions?

    For questions about your reservation, message the host through Airbnb. For ferry, golf cart or island questions, call (843) 342-8687 or use our contact page.
