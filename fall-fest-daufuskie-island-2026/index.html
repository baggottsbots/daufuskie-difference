    The menu

    Fresh off the dock.

    Please order at one of our bars. Every basket and sandwich comes with your choice of one side.

    Order at the bar

    Walk up to either bar to order food and drinks. Dine in under the oaks or take it to go.

    Getting here

    We're at Freeport Marina, where the ferry lands. The ride from Broad Creek Marina on Hilton Head takes about 45 minutes.

    Music with your meal

    Bands play on the deck most afternoons. See who's playing.
